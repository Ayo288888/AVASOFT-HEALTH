### What I'm Building & Working On

* **AVASOFT-HEALTH & AfroCare-Dx (1.18M Dataset)**  
  Creator and lead developer of an AI multi-agent clinical triage system designed for low-resource primary healthcare. I curated and open-sourced **AfroCare-Dx**—a harmonized 1.18 million record clinical dataset combining DDXPlus, Kaggle 773, AfriMed-QA v2, and SymCAT. The engine pairs a Stage 1 multi-label `DeBERTa-v3-large` classifier with Stage 2 WHO AFRO / NCDC clinical LLM reasoning, Groq Whisper voice intake, and Shannon entropy safety scoring.

* **Scarce**  
  Technical Team Lead and Founding Frontend Engineer building a high-performance fashion recommendation platform focused on fluid user interaction, dynamic filtering, and web performance.

* **Multimodal Forensics & Deepfake Detection (PhishGuard)**  
  Engineering a multi-layered security verification platform utilizing computer vision (YOLO / EfficientNet), audio analysis (Wav2Vec2), and text classification to detect synthetic media, audio deepfakes, and structural URL phishing attempts.

* **Open-Source Medical NLP Models**  
  Developed and published symptom classification models on Hugging Face (`Iloriayomide/Symptom_Prediction` and BioBERT variants) for clinical entity extraction and symptom outcome prediction.

* **n8n AI Cold Email Generator**  
  Created an automated outreach workflow using n8n orchestration, web scraping hooks, and LLM prompt engineering to generate context-aware, personalized email campaigns.
